# Contributing

This repository is a **read-only mirror**, automatically published from Give It
Smaller's private monorepo on each release. Pull requests and issues opened here
are **not** monitored and will not be merged.

Development happens in the private source repository. For bug reports or feature
requests, contact us via https://giveitsmaller.com.

## Developer Certificate of Origin (DCO)

Commits to the source repository are made under the
[Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/): contributors
certify authorship and their right to submit the work under this project's
Apache-2.0 license by signing off their commits (`git commit -s`).
